The Inverted Index

An inverted index maps every term to the list of documents containing it, called a postings list. Building one means tokenizing each document, normalizing the tokens, then recording the document id and often the position of each occurrence. Postings are kept sorted so lists can be merged quickly, and skip pointers speed up intersection. Positional postings allow phrase queries. Compared with scanning every document, an inverted index makes search proportional to the size of the answer rather than the size of the collection.
